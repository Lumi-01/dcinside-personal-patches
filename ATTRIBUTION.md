# Related implementation

The optional home-section and page-indicator behavior, plus the ad-dimension cleanup, were informed by the GPLv3 [AmpleReVanced DC Inside patches](https://github.com/AmpleReVanced/revanced-patches), whose published target is DC Inside 5.3.4. This repository adapts the concepts to verified 5.3.6 classes and resources. It does not bundle the Ample runtime or claim compatibility with all of its features.
